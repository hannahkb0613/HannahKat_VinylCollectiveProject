About Us | Our History

Our journey has been filled with learning and growth, and we are proud to have established a safe space where music enthusiasts can connect, share stories, and celebrate their love for vinyl. Hannah Kat's Vinyl Collective began as a small dream from our founder Hannah Bauer, originating from a deep love for art and music that blossomed into a carefully curated vinyl supply! What started as a personal collection of vinyl records from all over the world quickly transformed into a desire to share that experience with others. Our dedicated vinyl crew always ensures that each record we offer is in excellent condition and we continually seek out rare finds to keep our collection fresh and exciting. No matter what, our main focus is to continue on providing musical selections that accommodate all musical tastes!
