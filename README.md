# Hannah Kat's Vinyl Collective

**Online and In-Person Vinyl Store**

---

## About

*Note: Hannah Kat's Vinyl Collective is a fictional business created for a web development project; the details below are made up to give the site a realistic context.*

Welcome to Hannah Kat's Vinyl Collective, a multi-service music destination for all things vinyl! We believe that music is a universal language that will always bring people together no matter what. Our passion for music fuels everything we do, and we want to share the sounds we have found with the world. Our shop takes pride in curating unique selections that cater to everyone's taste, ensuring that you find something special every time you visit our online or in-person store. 

Our extensive vinyl collection features a vast and diverse range of genres like hip-hop to jazz and features records from eras containing the oldies but goodies all the way to today's top hits. In times like these, a little connection goes a long way and we hope to bridge even more gaps with our sounds! Located in the heart of the NYC music district, whether audiophiles or just embarking on building a record collection, our store is here to serve in finding you the perfect sounds to complement your lifestyle. All are welcome to relax, browse, and shop!

## How to Run
1. Clone the repository
2. Start a local MySQL server and run the SQL setup script to create the database and tables
3. Create a `database.inc` file with your database location, username, password, and database name
4. Serve the project with PHP (e.g. `php -S localhost:8000`) and open `index.php` in your browser
